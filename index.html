SEVEN AT A TIME

Practice until it sticks.

Practice questions: Answer each question. A wrong answer must be corrected before you can continue. At the end of seven, repeat only the questions you missed. Keep going until every question has been answered without a mistake on its latest attempt.

Matching: Match every question to an answer and submit the set. If any are wrong, repeat the entire same set until you get a perfect score. Answers can be reused when questions share the same correct answer.

Each mode covers all 36 items independently, in random batches of seven. The final batch contains one item. Progress saves on this browser; switching modes keeps your place.

Start this mode again?

This clears this mode’s saved progress and reshuffles its questions. Your other mode keeps its progress.
